## What attorney review means

Before a jurisdiction module is published, a licensed attorney reviews it and signs off, and that reviewer's identity and signing date are recorded permanently. Each lesson shows the date its citations were last verified against the primary source. That review is a quality control on our teaching materials. It is not a legal opinion addressed to you, and the reviewing attorney is not your attorney.

## Law changes

Statutes, regulations, agency guidance and court decisions change, sometimes without notice. A lesson that was accurate on its verification date may be out of date when you read it. Confirm any requirement against the current primary source, or with counsel, before you act on it.

## Compliance is your responsibility

Completing a RELI course does not make you or your organisation compliant with any law, and is not a defence to any claim, charge or audit. Employers remain responsible for their own policies, decisions and record-keeping. Where a course describes what the law generally requires, your circumstances, collective agreements, contracts, federal preemption and local ordinances may change the answer.
